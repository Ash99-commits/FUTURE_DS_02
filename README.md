## 🧾 Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive analysis of Facebook Ads Campaign performance, evaluating multiple campaigns across demographic segments, time periods, and spending efficiency. The interactive dashboard highlights key metrics such as impressions, clicks, conversions, and total expenditure to uncover patterns and support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 📂 Dataset Description

🎯 Metric Columns :-

**Total Impressions**: Total number of times ads were displayed to users.  
**Total Clicks**: Number of times users clicked on the ads.  
**Total Spent**: Total advertising cost incurred for the campaign.  
**Total Conversion**: Total number of successful actions (e.g., sign-ups or purchases) from ad clicks.  
**Approved Conversion**: Number of validated or approved conversions after review.  
**Average CTR (%)**: Percentage of impressions that resulted in clicks (Click-Through Rate).  
**Average CPA ($)**: Average cost incurred for acquiring one approved conversion (Cost per Acquisition).  
**Avg. Total CR (%)**: Conversion rate calculated as total conversions divided by total clicks.  
**Avg. Approved CR (%)**: Approved conversion rate calculated as approved conversions divided by total clicks.  

🧩 Filter Columns :-

**Gender**: Filter to view ad performance by male or female audience.  
**Age Group**: Filter to analyze results across different age brackets.  
**Campaign ID**: Filter to compare performance metrics of specific ad campaigns.  
**Date**: Filter to analyze campaign performance across selected reporting dates.

## 🔍Key Insights

- Campaign ID 1178 dominated performance — driving ≈89% of impressions, 82% of clicks, and 64% of approved conversions.  
- Male audience (30–34 age group) delivered the highest engagement with 34.53 M impressions and 4.2 K clicks.  
- Overall CTR was 0.015%, indicating low click engagement relative to impressions.  
- Peak conversions occurred on August 23 (263 conversions), showing strong mid-campaign performance.  
- Average CPA = $9.32 and Approved CR = 11.23%, reflecting moderate cost efficiency and conversion quality.  
- Males aged 30–34 received the highest spend ($7.4K) and impressions (34.5M), dominating campaign focus.  
- Females aged 35–39 had the highest total conversion rate (39.71%) and 30–34 led in approved conversions (18.22%), outperforming male counterparts.  
- 74.7% of approved conversions occurred on weekdays, indicating stronger weekday engagement.  
- Monday led with 18M impressions (22.46%), followed by Sunday at 13M (16.52%), suggesting optimal ad visibility early and late in the week.  
- Males aged 30–34 generated 4.2K clicks, aligning with their high spend and impression share.
